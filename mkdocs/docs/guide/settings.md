# Account Settings

Open **Settings** from the account menu in the top right.

## Account

Change your profile picture by hovering over it and choosing an image. Pictures are stored as lossless WebP. Update your username, email and password; an administrator may lock the username or email.

## Appearance

Choose a color theme (Cerberus, Catppuccin or Arch Linux) in light or dark mode. Accessibility options include a text size setting and a high contrast mode. These settings are saved in your browser.

## Storage

Shows how much space your documents and uploaded files use, and your storage quota if an administrator has set one.

## API keys

Create, regenerate and revoke keys for the [Render API](../api/index.md). The page shows your rate limit, how many keys you have out of your allowance, today's requests against your daily quota, and a usage chart for the last hour, day or week. A new key is shown only once, so copy it when it appears.

## Devices

Lists the Typst Desktop apps signed in to your account and whether they are online. Revoking a device signs it out and closes its live sync immediately.
