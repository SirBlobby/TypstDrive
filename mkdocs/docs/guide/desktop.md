# Typst Desktop

[Typst Desktop](https://github.com/SirBlobby/typst-desktop) is a companion app for Linux that keeps your documents as ordinary files on your computer and syncs them with TypstDrive.

## What it does

- Write Typst offline, with a live preview and the same kind of editor as the web app.
- Browse your TypstDrive folders, documents and projects, including things shared with you.
- Download the ones you want onto your computer and keep them in sync.
- Edit together with others live when you are online, with comments, suggestions and version history.
- Resolve conflicts when the same file changed in two places.
- Folder colors you choose in TypstDrive show in the app, and the other way around.

## Connecting the app

1. In Typst Desktop, open **Settings** and enter the address of your TypstDrive server.
2. Choose **Sign in**. Your browser opens TypstDrive.
3. Sign in as usual. TypstDrive shows a one-time key.
4. Paste the key into the app.

The app now shows your account picture and your TypstDrive files.

## Managing devices

Every connected app is listed in **Settings → Devices** in TypstDrive, with whether it is online. Revoke a device to sign it out straight away, for example if a laptop is lost.

## Installing

Download a package for Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch or an AppImage from the [Typst Desktop releases](https://github.com/SirBlobby/typst-desktop/releases).

Typst Desktop is also a community project and is not affiliated with the official Typst project.
