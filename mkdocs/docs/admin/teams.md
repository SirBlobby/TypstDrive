# Team spaces

**Site Settings → Team Spaces** controls [team spaces](../guide/teams.md).

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Allow team spaces | On | When off, team drives are hidden. Nothing is deleted, so turning it back on brings them back. |
| Who can create teams | Everyone | Everyone, or administrators only. |
| Teams each person can own | 5 | `0` means no limit. Administrators are not limited. |
| Members per team | 50 | Including the owner. `0` means no limit. |

**All teams** lists every team with its owner and number of members. You can delete any team along with its drive.

Team storage is shown and limited on the [Storage](storage.md) page.
